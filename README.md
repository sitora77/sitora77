# Hi, I'm sitora77 ⚓

I am building a portfolio at the intersection of **maritime systems, industrial
engineering, supply-chain resilience, and intelligent transportation**.

My undergraduate background is in **Port, Waterway and Coastal Engineering**.
During a freight-forwarding internship, I supported maritime operations and
marine cargo insurance work. That experience led me to explore how transparent
data analysis, simulation, and optimisation can improve operational decisions.

## Featured project

### [HarborShield](https://github.com/sitora77/harborshield)

A risk-aware maritime cargo insurance and intermodal logistics decision-support
prototype.

- Monte Carlo cargo-loss and delay simulation
- CVaR95 tail-risk analysis
- Cost–risk–time–carbon route comparison
- Cross-scenario resilience testing
- OR-Tools integer container allocation
- Official monthly vessel-arrival data from Singapore's MPA
- Explicit separation of public, simulated, and planned data

## What I am learning

## Research approach

I value transparent assumptions, reproducible experiments, traceable data, and
clear limitations. Portfolio models should support learning and decision analysis
without being presented as validated operational or actuarial systems before the
evidence exists.
